About The Project

Bridging Botanical Heritage & Modern Web Technology

HerbIT is an educational botanical technology platform designed to document, preserve, and share knowledge of medicinal plants in a simple, responsive, full-stack environment.

Traditional Wisdom

Learn time-tested wellness uses and traditional applications of revered Indian herbs like Tulsi, Neem, Turmeric, and Ashwagandha.

Botanical Taxonomy

Access accurate scientific nomenclature, classification categories, and specific plant anatomical parts used in herbal remedies.

Cultivation Guides

Gain actionable insights on sunlight, soil requirements, watering needs, and environmental care to grow these herbs in your home garden.
