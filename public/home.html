About HerbIT

Preserving Botanical Wisdom for the Modern Era

HerbIT is an educational virtual herbal garden created to bridge centuries of traditional wellness heritage with modern full-stack web technology.

Traditional Herbal Knowledge

Learn time-tested wellness practices and classical applications of revered Indian herbs like Tulsi, Neem, Turmeric, and Ashwagandha, documented with respectful adherence to Ayurvedic traditions.

Accurate Botanical Taxonomy

Explore precise scientific classifications (family, genus, species), anatomical parts used (leaves, roots, rhizomes), and categorized therapeutic properties organized in a searchable catalog.

Practical Home Cultivation

Receive actionable instructions on sunlight requirements, soil drainage, watering regimens, and climate suitability to cultivate healing botanical specimens right in your balcony or backyard.

Our Guiding Mission: "Grow Knowledge. Nurture Nature."

By digitizing herbal knowledge into an accessible, interactive digital sanctuary, HerbIT empowers students, researchers, and home gardeners to understand, celebrate, and preserve our natural biodiversity.

"How often should I water my Tulsi plant during monsoon season?"
Santhamithra: Tulsi requires well-draining loamy soil and moderate watering. During monsoon season, natural humidity is elevated: ensure your pot drainage holes are free of obstruction and allow the top 1-2 inches of soil to dry out between waterings to prevent root rot.
"What is the best part of Turmeric used for wellness teas?"
Santhamithra: The underground rhizome (freshly grated or dried powder) is traditionally infused with hot water or warm milk along with a pinch of black pepper to aid absorption.
